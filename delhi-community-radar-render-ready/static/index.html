STEP 1 / Tell us what's wrongHI · EN · HINGLISH

Delhi Community Radar is not an emergency response service. For emergencies, contact 112.

Location: not set. Tap My location or tap the map.
Before you submit: privacy
  • Location is required (GPS or a tap on the map) so nearby reports can be grouped and shown in the right area.
  • Your description and any photo are analysed by an AI service (Claude by Anthropic) when AI mode is on. Photos are not saved by this app. Phone numbers, e-mails and 12-digit ID numbers are masked automatically.
  • If you report someone elderly, ill or otherwise vulnerable, do not include their name, phone number or house address. Their exact location is never shown publicly, only an approximate area of about 1 km.
  • Don't include personal details you don't need. Reports are held in server memory only in this prototype and may disappear when the server restarts.

Submitting creates a draft complaint for you to review and send yourself. It is not sent to any office.

How it works

Five steps from a shaky voice note to a complaint you can send.

01

Capture

Voice, photo or text, in Hindi, English or Hinglish. Location by GPS or a tap on the map.

02

Understand

AI reads the report (and the photo) to find the issue type, severity, locality and whether vulnerable people are at risk.

03

Cluster

Reports of the same problem within 700 m merge into one, and the pile-up raises its priority.

04

Suggest authority

AI identifies the relevant body (MCD, DJB, PWD, DPCC, RWA or NGO) and prepares a formal complaint draft. You review it and submit it yourself. Nothing is sent automatically.

05

Match

Nearby volunteers with the right skill are suggested. In this prototype they are SAMPLE profiles: no real volunteer is contacted or has accepted.
