How it works

Five steps from a shaky voice note to the right desk.

01

Capture

Voice, photo or text, in Hindi, English or Hinglish. Location by GPS or a tap on the map.

02

Understand

AI reads the report (and the photo) to find the issue type, severity, locality and whether vulnerable people are at risk.

03

Cluster

Reports of the same problem within 700 m merge into one, and the pile-up raises its priority.

04

Route

Matched to the right body (MCD, DJB, PWD, DPCC, RWA or NGO), with a formal complaint already drafted for the citizen to send.

05

Match

Nearby volunteers with the right skill are suggested, so help can start before the office responds.
